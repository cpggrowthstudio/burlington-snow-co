      Questions
      
Good to know

      

        
How much does snow removal cost in Burlington?
Our season plans run November to April and include the driveway, walkway and front steps. A townhouse is $499, a single car driveway is $649, a double car driveway is $799 and a large driveway (5 or more cars) is $999, plus HST. Commercial sites are quoted per site.

        
Which parts of Burlington do you serve?
All of Burlington, from Aldershot to Burloak Drive and from Lake Ontario to Dundas Street, plus Alton Village. That includes Tyandaga, Brant Hills, Headon Forest, Millcroft, Tansley, Orchard, Palmer, Downtown, Roseland and Shoreacres.

        
When do you come?
Every time 5 cm or more falls, November to April. You're cleared automatically. You don't need to call.

        
What counts as the walkway?
The path from your driveway to your front door, plus the front steps.

        
Do you clear the snowbank the city plow leaves?
Yes. The end of your driveway is cleared on every visit.

        
How do I pay?
Half when you sign up and half on January 15.

        
Are you insured?
Yes. Burlington Snow Co. carries liability insurance for every property we clear.
